# E0 — Safety Hazard Register

**Status: ANALYSIS, not mitigation. Block E: NOT EXECUTED.**

Severity scale: **CRITICAL** (injury/fire/hardware death credible), **MAJOR**
(wrong behavior, damage, or blocked qualification), **MINOR** (nuisance,
measurement error). "Blocks E" = Block E cannot PASS with it open.
"Blocks F" = first power is forbidden with it open. No hazard is marked
mitigated by a mocked/host test — only by a cited physical control.

Residual risk statement: even a PASS Block E leaves H-09/H-11 (reset/power-up
levels) and H-12 (stall current) open by construction — they need bounded power
to observe. Block E's job is to make first power bounded and unsurprising, not
to prove powered behavior. The Block F register owns them.
